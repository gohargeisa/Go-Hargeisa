## Full description
_(Play Console limit: 4000 characters)_

```
Go Hargeisa is your complete guide to Hargeisa, Somaliland — discover, book, and explore the capital with confidence.

DISCOVER THE CITY
Browse hotels, restaurants, cafés, tourist attractions, upcoming events, and essential city services (hospitals, banks, pharmacies, and more) — all in one place, organized and easy to search.

BOOK WITH CONFIDENCE
Request hotel bookings directly through the app, read genuine reviews from other travelers and locals, and see real ratings before you decide.

REVIEWS YOU CAN TRUST
Leave your own reviews with photos, mark reviews as helpful, and see verified owner replies — every listing shows a real average rating built from real visits.

SAVE YOUR FAVORITES
Build your own shortlist of favorite hotels, restaurants, and places to visit, and pick up right where you left off across sessions.

GET THERE EASILY
Every listing connects straight to Google Maps for directions, one-tap calling, WhatsApp messaging, and email — no copying and pasting numbers or addresses.

NEARBY PLACES
See what else is close to where you are looking — nearby hotels, restaurants, cafés, and attractions sorted by real distance.

BUILT FOR HARGEISA
Available in English, Arabic, and Somali, with full right-to-left support for Arabic. Opening hours show real-time "Open Now" / "Closes Soon" status in your own timezone.

WORKS EVEN WITH A WEAK CONNECTION
A dedicated offline screen keeps you informed and automatically reconnects the moment your connection returns — no confusing browser error pages.

WHO IT'S FOR
Whether you're visiting Hargeisa for the first time, a member of the diaspora returning home, or a local looking for what's new around the city, Go Hargeisa is built to be your everyday companion.

Go Hargeisa is an independent guide connecting you with local, independently owned businesses — we're not the operator of any hotel, restaurant, or venue listed.
```
